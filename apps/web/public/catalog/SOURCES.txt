ANAN development imagery
Local files for the storefront demo. They are not photographs of the fictional
Solara, Tekora, Briza, Novent, Lumio, or Ardent models, and they are not
Samsung, LG, or Bosch campaign art.

Storefront use
- Category tiles, subcategory tiles, the homepage hero, split promos, and most
  product cards use the original vis-*.webp drawings below.
- product-fridge-retro.webp is used only for the retro single-door demo product
  and the single-door subcategory tile.
- product-laptop.webp is used for the laptop demo product and the computers tile.
- A site-wide development notice states that prices, stock, and brands are demo data.
  Product cards do not repeat a long disclaimer. Alt text still says when an image
  is not the listed model.
