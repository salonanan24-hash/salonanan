# ANAN visual redesign

The routes, filters, and catalog already work. The weak impression comes from presentation, not from missing pages.

## Causes

- The page background is a large gray canvas, so every section looks like a form on a prototype.
- Category tiles crop lifestyle photos (`object-cover`): kitchens, lounges, and installed air conditioners stand in for the product.
- The hero paints marketing text on top of those room photos, with poor contrast and no product focus.
- Product cards repeat a demo badge and a long photo disclaimer, so the price and the appliance compete with notes.
- Category and brand heroes are either a cropped room banner or a text box. Subcategory tiles are faint line drawings that look empty.
- Homepage sections share one pattern: heading, then a white card row. There is no change of scale or color.
- Several television cards share one small photograph. That is a data limit. The layout must not stretch those cards to hide it.

## Direction

White page, ink text `#172033`, red `#E52629` for price and primary actions, blue `#0C5695` for navigation and secondary actions. Borders stay light. Shadows stay small. Radius stays around 8–12px. The original logo does not change.

Hebrew uses Heebo. Arabic uses Noto Sans Arabic.

Category and product imagery is a product on white. Room photos leave the category tiles, product cards, and hero. Marketing sentences stay in HTML so Hebrew and Arabic can change. A preview label is allowed. No invented discount or delivery promise.

Sections alternate on purpose: product hero, category tiles, carousel, split promos, another carousel, a blue strip, brand names, an editorial block, then benefits. A short catalog keeps normal card widths. Empty brands get a designed introduction and an empty listing, not fabricated products.
